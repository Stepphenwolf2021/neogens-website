## Many years of the same problem, in different organizations

Neo Gens is not an academic practice. Every line below was a commercial or public-accountability problem before it was a knowledge problem — a budget to defend, an audience to win back, a board to answer to. That is the half most knowledge-management advice leaves out, and it is the half that decides whether the work survives contact with a real institution.

### Thailand Creative & Design Center (TCDC) — founding team, thirteen years

Directed information technology and facility management for Thailand's national design centre: digital library systems, knowledge-access tools and creative resource collections used by tens of thousands of designers, students and entrepreneurs. Then founded and led the centre's **Creative Business Development** division — using Service Design Thinking to turn that knowledge into commercial value for government agencies, corporates, SMEs and start-ups.

### Ubonratchathani Public Library — Project Director

Redevelopment of a provincial public library into a 21st-century library concept. Winner of **Thailand's Best Public Library award, 2011**.

### Bangkok General Post Office — Project Leader

Adaptive reuse of an 80-year-old heritage building into TCDC's flagship knowledge and design space.

### MQDC — Senior Vice President, Creative Lab, and museum planner

Museum planner on a large-scale museum programme spanning a **cultural museum and a natural-history museum**, while leading experience-led value creation across the developer's flagship projects. A museum built inside a commercial development has to earn every visitor — there is no assumption of attendance to fall back on.

### Siam Piwat · museum facility and technology

Advisory on standards benchmarked against global museum practice — HVAC, humidity control, fire suppression, lighting and security — and the technology framework for visitor experience.

### GLG expert network — since 2017

Subject-matter expert on museums and cultural venues for international consulting houses and investment firms researching the regional cultural-venue sector.

### TK Park · Thailand Knowledge Park (under OKMD) and the ThaiHealth Learning Center — 2018–2019

Our **CoFuture Service Design Thinking** approach, run with the C-level and management teams of both institutions to build organisational strategy *with* them rather than deliver it to them. Interviews with the leadership of every department first, then working sessions where the organisation's shared future was assembled from what each of them said — and authored by their own team. Strategy and positioning work: no graph was built, because the technology was not ready in 2018.
