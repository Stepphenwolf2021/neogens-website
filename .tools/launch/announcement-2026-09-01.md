## 2 · โพสต์ LinkedIn ฉบับอังกฤษ

neogens.co is live today.

Neo Gens is a Modern Knowledge Management practice. Our first practice area is museums, libraries and archives.

We start there for three plain reasons. They have practised this discipline longer than anyone. They set the standards the rest of the field borrows. And it is where the cost of getting AI wrong is highest.

Everything an organisation knows sits in boxes, and there are thousands of them — the research you pay for again every year, the production steps you would never publish, the judgement your people built up over decades. Every box is full. Nothing runs between them.

So the question worth asking before you field an AI is not how clever it is. It is whether it can read every box, and whether you can check which box each answer came out of.

Because every answer an AI gives in your name is your reputation.

We are not against AI. We use it hard for what it is good at. We just do not let it be the place where truth lives.

The site sets out how we think, what we have actually built, and what we cannot do yet. English and Thai.
