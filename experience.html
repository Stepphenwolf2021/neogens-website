MKM for Museums & Libraries · 02 — What changes for the visitor and the reader

The holdings don't change. The paths through them do.

Once concepts, objects, people and evidence are modelled as a graph, a route through the collection becomes a question rather than a construction project — and can be recomposed endlessly from scholarship you already own. In a gallery that route is a visit. In a reading room it is an answer that finally crosses the catalogue, the archive and the digital repository in one move.

Same exhibit, same evening, three visitors
Standing in front of the same object

This is not three exhibitions. It is one concept, with depth held as a property and different relationships followed for each visitor. Illustrative example.

Experience 01

A different thread every visit

The permanent collection stays permanent — but the route through it can change monthly, entered through trade, through repair and reuse, through a single decade.

Experience 02

The stored collection joins in

Most of what you hold will never fit in a gallery. Connected, those objects still take part — as the thing the object in front of you turns out to be related to.

Experience 03

Every question becomes an asset

Questions the collection can't answer are recorded against the item. Repeated often enough, they become a research agenda ranked by real public curiosity.

Experience 04 · reading room

A reference answer that shows its working

A reader's enquiry returns your own holdings, your own finding aids and your own subject expertise — with the trail attached — instead of a ranked list that stops at whatever happens to be catalogued in one system.

And the guardrail that makes it safe to do at all
A visitor-facing AI is not an exhibit. Exhibitions close. Answers don't.

Every answer it gives is an institutional statement, issued in your name, hundreds of times a day, without a curator reading a word of it first. An institution that holds society's knowledge cannot let statements without provenance go out under its name — and that obligation doesn't lapse because the statement was generated rather than written.

Which is why the layer comes first, and why the rules on it are strict: sensitive material stays inside your boundary, contested attributions stay contested, and only a curator promotes a claim to a fact. Prepared properly, the constraint becomes the feature — an AI that is structurally incapable of overclaiming is something no general assistant can offer.
